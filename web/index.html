Choosing a hospital for a delivery, a knee replacement or dialysis? Star ratings blur thousands of OPD visits together. Bedside Manner reads the Google reviews that mention your procedure at the hospitals near you — the lowest-rated first, then the newest — and charts what patients complained about, when, and whether the hospital replied.

Reviews are individual experiences, not medical evidence. Talk to your doctor, ask the hospital for a written estimate and your insurer for cashless approval before admission.

Case notes — what we looked up

    Bedside Manner never rates or ranks a hospital. It counts and quotes public Google reviews (names of doctors and staff removed) so you can ask better questions. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital — don't compare.
